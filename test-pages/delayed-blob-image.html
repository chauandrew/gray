blob: src assigned after a delayed fetch

Pattern: an <img> starts with no src at all. ~1s later, JS builds a Blob (simulating a resolved fetch()) and assigns img.src = URL.createObjectURL(blob) — no new node, just an attribute change on an existing element.

Expected: nothing for the first second (no src, nothing to flatten or box). Once the blob: src is assigned, it should flatten to neutral gray immediately, then upgrade to your chosen box color shortly after — same two-stage behavior as the data-URI case.

Bug if: after assignment, the image stays at neutral gray and never upgrades to the chosen color — it would mean the attributeFilter: ["src"] watch isn't catching src changes on an existing node.

Real-world source: client-side image processing / lazy-loaders that fetch then blob-assign.
